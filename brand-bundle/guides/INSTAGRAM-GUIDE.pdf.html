Instagram Posting Guide

Posting Schedule

Consistency matters more than frequency. Aim for 3-4 posts per week and daily stories during event weeks. Here's a recommended weekly rhythm:

DayPost TypeWhat to Share
MondayBehind-the-ScenesPrepping for the week, testing recipes, kitchen moments
WednesdayProduct SpotlightClose-up of a specific item with description and price
FridayEvent / Order ReminderUpcoming event, order deadline, what's available
SundayPersonal / GratitudeThank customers, share a family story, reflect on the week
Pro tip: You don't need to post every day. One great photo with a genuine caption beats five mediocre posts. Quality over quantity.

Content Types That Work

1. Process Shots (Best Engagement)

People love watching food being made. Dough rising, rolls going into the oven, frosting being spread. These are your highest-engagement posts.

2. Finished Product Beauty Shots

Clean, well-lit photos of the final product. Natural light near a window is perfect. Show the texture — golden crust, soft interior, gooey brownies.

3. Order/Event Announcements

Clear, direct posts about what's available and how to order. Always include a photo. Put key details (date, price, how to order) in the first 2 lines.

4. Customer Moments

If customers share photos of your food at their gatherings, repost (with permission). This is powerful social proof.

5. Personal/Family Stories

Why you bake. Who taught you. What your family loves most. These build connection and loyalty.

  • Quick videos: 5-10 second clips of baking process, no editing needed
  • Repost customer stories: When tagged, always reshare
