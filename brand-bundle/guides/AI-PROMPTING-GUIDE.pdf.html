AI Prompting Guide
Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant to create on-brand content for your business.

How This Works

AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude can help you write Instagram captions, event descriptions, text messages to customers, and more. The key is giving them the right context about your brand so everything sounds like you, not a robot.

Below are ready-to-use prompts. Just copy, paste, and fill in the [brackets] with your specific details.

Quick tip: Always start a new conversation with the "Brand Context" prompt below. This teaches the AI who you are. Then ask it to write whatever you need.

The Brand Context Prompt

Copy and paste this at the start of every new conversation. It gives the AI everything it needs to write in your voice:

You are helping Kathleen, who runs Kathleen's Kitchen — a home bakery in Sandy, Utah. She bakes homemade breads and sweet treats from scratch for local pickup. Her brand voice is: - Warm, personal, and friendly — like texting a neighbor - First person ("I" not "we") — Kathleen IS the brand - Humble and genuine — proud of her work but never boastful - Casual but not sloppy — approachable, not corporate Her products include: - Honey Wheat Bread ($10/loaf) - Soft French Bread ($8/loaf) - White Bread ($8/loaf) - Texas Sheet Cake ($25/pan, $15/half) - Brownies ($15/dozen, $25/pan) - Banana Bars ($15/dozen, $25/pan) - Cinnamon Rolls ($20/dozen) - Caramel Rolls ($25/dozen) - Orange Rolls ($22/dozen) Customers order through her website (kathleens.kitchen) or by texting (801) 808-9888. Pickup is in Sandy, Utah. Her Instagram is @kathleens.kitchen.ut Always write in her voice. Keep it genuine and warm. Never use corporate words like "artisanal," "curated," or "elevated." Never use more than 5-8 hashtags.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

Instagram Caption — Event Announcement

Write an Instagram caption announcing my [event name] event. I'm baking [list items]. Pickup is [date and time] in Sandy. Orders close [deadline]. Keep it warm and exciting but not pushy. Include a call to action to text me or use the link in my bio.

Instagram Caption — Product Photo

Write an Instagram caption for a photo of my [product name]. [Describe the photo briefly — e.g., "fresh out of the oven, golden brown, on a wooden cutting board"]. Make it mouth-watering but genuine. Include 5-6 hashtags.

Instagram Caption — Behind the Scenes

Write an Instagram caption for a behind-the-scenes photo of me [describe what you're doing — e.g., "kneading dough for 40 loaves of wheat bread"]. Make it feel real and personal. Maybe mention how my kitchen smells or how much I love this part.

Instagram Caption — Thank You Post

Write a thank-you Instagram caption. I just finished my [event name] event and made [number] [items]. I want to thank my customers and tease that [upcoming event] is coming next. Keep it heartfelt and short.

Instagram Story Text

Give me 3 short Instagram story captions (under 20 words each) for [describe the moment — e.g., "cinnamon rolls going into the oven" or "packaging up orders for tomorrow's pickup"]. Make them casual and fun.

Event Description for Website

Write a short event description (3-4 sentences) for my website. The event is [event name] on [date]. I'm offering [list items with prices]. Pickup is [time and location details]. Keep it clear and inviting.

Text Message to Customers

Write a short text message (under 160 characters) letting my customers know that [what you want to tell them — e.g., "Easter orders are now open" or "I have 3 extra loaves of wheat bread available today"]. Keep it super casual like a text to a friend.

Seasonal/Holiday Ideas

Give me 5 Instagram post ideas for [holiday/season — e.g., "Thanksgiving week" or "back to school"]. For each idea, give me the post type (photo, reel, story), what to photograph, and a one-line caption idea. Keep everything on brand for a home bakery.

Customer Reply Templates

Write 3 friendly text message replies for common customer questions: 1. Someone asking what I have available this week 2. Someone asking if I can do a custom order 3. Someone thanking me for their order Keep each under 2-3 sentences. Warm and personal.

Bio / About Text

Write a short bio for [where — e.g., "my Instagram" or "a local market application" or "a holiday flyer"]. It should explain that I run a home bakery in Sandy, Utah, I bake everything from scratch, and how to order. Max [number] words.

Tips for Better Results

1
Always paste the Brand Context first. This is the most important step. Without it, the AI will sound generic.
2
Be specific. "Write a caption for my cinnamon rolls cooling on a wire rack" gets better results than "Write a caption for my food."
3
Ask for options. Say "Give me 3 versions" and pick the one that feels most like you.
4
Edit it. The AI gives you a starting point. Change words, add your own details, make it yours. It should sound like you, not like a computer.
5
Say "more casual" or "warmer" or "shorter." If the first draft isn't right, just tell the AI what to fix. It'll adjust.
