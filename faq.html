Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, pickup, and our baked goods.

Browse our upcoming events on the main page, pick your items, and check out securely online. You'll receive a text from Kathleen to confirm your order details.
We offer pickup in Sandy, Utah on the event date. Local delivery is also available within the Sandy and Draper area for a small additional fee. Delivery details are confirmed when Kathleen texts you.
Each event has limited spots that fill up fast. We recommend ordering as soon as an event goes live for the best selection. Follow us on Instagram @kathleens.kitchen.ut to get notified first.
Absolutely! We love helping with birthdays, baby showers, family reunions, and other special occasions. Use our custom inquiry form on the main page to tell us about your event and we'll work something out.
Most of our items contain wheat, dairy, eggs, and sugar. Some items contain nuts or chocolate. If you have specific allergy concerns, please reach out and we'll be happy to provide detailed ingredient information for any item.
We operate as a Utah Cottage Food producer under Utah's cottage food laws. This means our kitchen is a home kitchen and is not inspected by the Department of Agriculture, as permitted by state law.
Yes! Our rolls and breads freeze beautifully. Wrap them tightly in plastic wrap or foil and freeze within 24 hours of receiving them. To enjoy, thaw at room temperature or warm in the oven at 350°F for a few minutes.
We accept all major credit and debit cards through our secure Square checkout system. Payment is collected when you place your order online.
