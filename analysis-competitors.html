Market Gap Analysis

Key Market Findings

Most saturated categories: Sourdough bread, custom sugar cookies, and custom cakes. These categories are crowded with cottage bakers across the Wasatch Front.

Least saturated categories: Dinner rolls, brownies/bars, and rice krispie treats. Very few home bakers are competing here at all.

Kathleen's differentiation: The product mix of classic rolls + brownies + bars + treats is genuinely differentiated from the market. Most cottage bakers trend toward artisan, gourmet, or specialty products (sourdough, French macarons, custom decorated goods).

The "classic comfort" niche is almost entirely unoccupied. Kathleen's Kitchen owns a lane that very few competitors are even attempting to enter.
