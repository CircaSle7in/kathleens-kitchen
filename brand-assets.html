Sticker Designs

Ready-to-print sticker layouts for packaging, bags, and boxes. High-resolution PNGs at 300 DPI, print-ready. Print on sticker paper from any home printer or send to a print shop like Sticker Mule or Avery.

How to print: Use Avery round labels (2" diameter, template 22807) for the round stickers. For rectangles, use Avery address labels (2" x 4", template 5163). Print on any inkjet or laser printer. For professional results, order custom stickers from StickerMule.com or Stickergiant.com.
